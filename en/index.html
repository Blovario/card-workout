FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

EVERYTHING
YOU NEED TO KNOW.

How does drawing cards work?+

DeckBurn shuffles a 52-card deck. Each suit maps to an exercise, and the card value sets the reps. Draw the next card whenever you're ready.

How can I play the same deck with friends?+

Share your session's six-character code. Friends can enter it in the app to get the same 52 cards in the same order. Everyone moves at their own pace.

How many reps are face cards and aces?+

Jack: 11 reps, Queen: 12, King: 13, and Ace: 14. Number cards count as their face value.

Is DeckBurn available on Android?+

The iPhone app is available on the App Store. Google Play is coming soon.

Do I need an account?+

No. You can open the app and start a workout without creating an account.
